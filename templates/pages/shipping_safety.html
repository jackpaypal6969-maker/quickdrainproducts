Shipping regime

Why there is no hazmat line on the receipt.

Enzymatic and bacterial drain products are not DOT-regulated corrosives. Carriers treat them as ordinary parcels: no hazmat surcharge, no ground-only restriction, no carrier refusal. Caustic and acid drain openers are the opposite{% if not any_hazmat %}, which is one reason there is none in this catalog{% endif %}.

Each product's class is listed in the table, straight from its formulation type. If that ever changes for a product, this page changes with it.

Corrosive products

Different rules for the products marked hazmat.

Anything marked Yes in the hazmat column is a DOT-regulated corrosive. The ordinary-parcel terms above do not apply to it.

{{ icon('alert', 20, 'text-warning shrink-0 mt-0.5') }}

Ground-only and carrier restrictions apply.

  • Ground service only: no air, so delivery takes longer and some destinations cannot be served.
  • Carrier rules limit where and how these parcels travel. PO boxes and some address types are not available.
  • Shipping for an order that includes one of these products may differ from the flat rate. The checkout total is final.

What counts

Anything with its own trap: sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, laundry standpipes and floor drains. A tub-shower combination is one drain. Skip the dishwasher; it drains through the kitchen sink.

Why twelve

The label reads “dosed for monthly use on any drain”. Twelve bottles keep one drain on that schedule for a year, so a box of 12 is one drain-year and your count is the number of boxes.

When it is not maintenance

A slow or backing-up drain is a diagnosis, not a dose. Call Quick Drain at {{ settings.phone_display }} for a camera inspection first.

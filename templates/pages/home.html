Read this before you buy

When a bottle is the wrong answer.

A maintainer keeps a clean line clean. It does not diagnose, and it does not fix these. If one of them is happening now, book the visit and use Drain Shot afterwards.

{{ icon('alert', 22) }}

Water standing or backing up

Something is physically in the line: grease, roots, scale or a broken pipe. A camera inspection shows which, before anyone quotes a fix.

{{ icon('refresh', 22) }}

Cleared last month, slow again

Clearing opens a hole; cleaning restores the pipe wall. A line that was only cleared often slows again; wall-to-wall jetting is how the pipe wall gets cleaned.

{{ icon('droplet', 22) }}

Gurgling, smells, more than one fixture

Several drains acting up together points past the trap — main line, vent or cesspool. That is a diagnostic, not a dose.

Short version: {{ settings.flat_shipping_cents|fmt_money }} flat to any US address, free once the order reaches {{ settings.free_shipping_threshold_cents|fmt_money_short }}, shipped from Long Island as an ordinary parcel.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States. Stripe's checkout page only accepts a ship-to address we can serve, so if it will not take yours, we cannot deliver there. We do not ship internationally.

Cost

  • {{ settings.flat_shipping_cents|fmt_money }} flat rate per order, whatever the pack size or quantity.
  • Free shipping when the order subtotal, after any discount, is {{ settings.free_shipping_threshold_cents|fmt_money_short }} or more. The cart shows how far you are from it.
  • Sales tax, where it applies, is calculated on Stripe's checkout page from the ship-to address.

Why it ships as an ordinary parcel

Quick Shot is an enzymatic formulation, not a caustic or acid drain opener. It is not a DOT-regulated corrosive, so there is no hazmat surcharge, no ground-only restriction and no signature requirement. Bottles are packed upright. Where a formulation requires it, a California Proposition 65 warning is shown on the product page; see Shipping & safety for what that means for each product.

When it ships

Orders leave Long Island within one to two business days, Monday to Friday, excluding federal holidays. Orders placed over a weekend go out the next business day. We email tracking as soon as the label is printed, and it also appears on your order page and in Find your order.

How long it takes

Most parcels arrive 2 to 5 business days after shipping within the Northeast and 3 to 7 business days elsewhere in the country. Those are the carrier's estimates, not a guarantee. If tracking has not moved for 5 business days, tell us and we chase it or replace it.

Address changes

We ship to the address entered on Stripe's checkout page exactly as entered, so check it before you pay. If you spot a mistake, contact us or call {{ settings.phone_display }} straight away; we can change it until the label prints. After that the carrier decides whether a redirect is possible. A parcel returned to us as undeliverable is refunded less the outbound shipping charge, or sent again once shipping is paid a second time.

Lost, damaged or stolen

A parcel damaged in transit or lost by the carrier is replaced at our cost; the refunds and returns policy has the details. If tracking shows delivered and you cannot find it, check with neighbours and any mail room, then let us know within 7 days and we work through it with the carrier.

Reorder reminders

Because one bottle is one drain-month, we know roughly when an order runs out. A few days before, we send one reminder email with a reorder link. It is sent once per order and can be ignored.

Drain Shot is a consumable, so the rules are short: sealed bottles come back, opened ones do not, and anything we got wrong is fixed at our cost.

30-day returns on unopened bottles

Any bottle still sealed can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a refund of what you paid for it. Multi-packs can be returned in part (four sealed bottles from a six-pack, say) and the refund is worked out per bottle. The original shipping charge is not refunded unless the return is our fault, and you cover return postage unless it is.

Opened, partly used or unsealed bottles cannot be returned or refunded. This is a liquid enzyme product: once the seal is broken we cannot resell it or know how it was stored.

Damaged, leaking, wrong or missing

If a parcel arrives damaged, a bottle has leaked, you received the wrong item or quantity, or tracking says delivered and nothing came, tell us as soon as you notice, within the 30-day window. Include a photo where there is something to photograph. We replace or refund, your choice, and nothing needs to be shipped back. If a parcel is lost in transit we replace it once the carrier confirms it is not coming.

How to start a return

  1. Signed in: open the order on your account page and use Request a return.
  2. Guest: use Find your order with the order number and email, then request the return from there.
  3. We reply within one business day with a return address, or with a replacement or refund confirmation when nothing needs to come back.
  4. Pack the bottles upright so they cannot knock together and send them with a tracked service. Keep the receipt until the refund lands.

How refunds are paid

Every refund goes back to the payment method used at checkout, issued through Stripe. We do not refund to a different card, by cheque or in cash; store credit is offered only if you ask for it. A refund is issued once the sealed bottles reach us and are checked, or of our decision when nothing is being returned. The card issuer then usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it. The refunded amount appears on your order page and is confirmed by email.

Discounts and free shipping

If a discount code applied to the order, the refund is for the discounted price you actually paid. Subscriptions

A subscription renewal that has already shipped is treated like any other order: sealed bottles are returnable within 30 days. To stop future renewals, contact us before the next billing date and the cancellation takes effect from that date.

Cancelling an order

Orders usually ship within one to two business days. To cancel, contact us or call {{ settings.phone_display }} straight away. If the parcel has not left, we cancel and refund in full; if it has, treat it as a return.

What this policy does not cover

A product that did what its label says but did not solve a problem the label never claimed to solve (a backup, a blocked line, roots) is not a defect. That drain needs a diagnostic visit, and we would rather you book one at quickdrainny.com than buy a second bottle.

Nothing here limits any right you have under New York consumer protection law.

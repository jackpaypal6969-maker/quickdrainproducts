{{ settings.app_name }} should be usable by everyone, including people who rely on a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or reduced motion. The target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA across the whole store, checkout included.

What is built in

  • Every page starts with a skip link to the main content, and every control can be reached and operated with the keyboard, including the cart drawer, the menu and the product photo gallery.
  • Form fields have visible labels, not placeholder text. Errors are shown in plain language next to the form and announced to assistive technology.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall. Icon-only buttons carry a text label for assistive technology.
  • Text meets the AA contrast ratio against the dark background, and the single accent colour is never the only thing that carries meaning.
  • Motion is limited to short fades and rises and switches off entirely when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Page structure uses real headings, lists and landmarks. The site works at 200% zoom and on a 320-pixel-wide screen without sideways scrolling.
  • Checkout runs on Stripe's hosted page, which Stripe maintains to its own accessibility standard.

Known limits

  • Safety data sheets and label files are supplied by the manufacturer as PDFs and may not be fully tagged. If you need the information in another format, ask and we will send it as plain text.
  • Product photographs carry short alt text. The label lines, dose and directions are reproduced as text on the product page, so nothing important lives only in an image.

Tell us what is not working

If any part of this site is hard to use, we want to know: the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you were using. Send it through the contact form or call {{ settings.phone_display }}. We reply within two business days and treat confirmed barriers as a priority fix.

How this is checked

Pages are checked with automated tooling and by hand with keyboard-only navigation and a screen reader before they go live, and again after any change to the layout. This statement is reviewed whenever the site changes materially; the date at the top shows the last review.
